# Tipping cascades in the Earth system

This case checks whether the framework can specify a prediction about a partially known coupled system whose possible futures depend on several sources of uncertainty.

- [Research problem](description.md): to understand what the case demands of the framework before reading its records.
- [Target system](target-system.yml): to know which subsystems the prediction covers.
- [Background laws](background.yml): to know which laws of the target the question admits, and on what warrant.
- [Phenomenon](phenomenon.yml): to know which outcome the prediction concerns.
- [Question](question.yml): to know which predictions an answer must deliver, and with what accuracy.
- [Network model](model.yml): to read the stylized network of four tipping elements on which the forecast runs.
- [Binding](binding.yml): to read how the elements, their probabilities, their times and the forcing are carried onto the Earth system.
- [Key of the tipped state](key-tipped-state.yml): to read how the regime of a subsystem is read from the state of its element.
- [Key of the warming](key-warming.yml): to read how the warming of the model maps onto global warming.
- [Candidate answer](answer.yml): to examine a candidate drawn from a published model result.
- [Assessment](assessment.yml): to learn which tests the candidate passes or fails, and why.
- [Operators](operators.yml): to find the operators that the records of this problem use beyond the canonical set.
- [Open issues](issues.md): to know which limitations of the framework the case still exposes.
- [Vocabulary](vocabulary.yml): to choose a new symbol without colliding with one already declared.
