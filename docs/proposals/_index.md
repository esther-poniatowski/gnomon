# Proposals

Holds the candidate designs that await a decision, each note gathering the alternatives for one open goal of the [project goals](../../TODO.md), until an [architecture decision](../adr/_index.md) records the choice.

> [!IMPORTANT] Entries and retired candidates
> In each note, an entry states the idea, the literature that it draws on, and the goal that it bears on, with a second goal when a part of the idea bears elsewhere. The entry stays until the work on its goal adopts or declines it. The statements cited as examples belong to the [adversarial statements](../../examples/adversarial-statements.md).
>
> An entry recovered from the retired design notes names its source file at commit 9e138e2, under **Retired source**.

- [Proposals for research actions](research-actions.md): weighs whether the activities of the registry derive from a smaller set of constructors
- [Proposals for the relation registry](relation-registry.md): weighs the forms of the relations that one registry declares between research units
- [Proposals for the derivation framework](derivation-framework.md): weighs the formal objects that state a derivation, its steps and its results
- [Proposals for the research corrections](research-corrections.md): weighs the record forms required by the corrections of the Mixture pilot
- [Proposals for the definitions framework](definitions-framework.md): weighs the forms of the full definition object to which a concept record may link
- [Proposals for the vocabulary records](vocabulary-records.md): weighs the schema of the concept record
- [Proposals for status propagation](status-propagation.md): weighs which revisions of a claim mark its dependents for review
- [Proposals for the second round of inquiry records](inquiry-records-round-2.md): weighs the records that close the gaps of the type graph of activities
- [Proposals for the problem record](problem-record.md): weighs the extensions of the problem record
- [Proposals for the classification method](classification-method.md): weighs the routes that build a taxonomic layer on a qualitative feature system
- [Proposals for the exposition family](exposition-family.md): weighs the units by which an exposition projects research records
- [Proposals for the model design](model-design.md): weighs the revisions of the model design
- [Proposals for the assembly schema](assembly-schema.md): weighs the schema of the assembly of a level
