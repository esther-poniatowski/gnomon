# gnomon documentation

Directs a reader of `gnomon` to the folder that serves one task: specifying and checking an inquiry, deciding a design question, or consulting the external literature.

> [!IMPORTANT] Note conventions
> The frontmatter keys, the tag vocabulary and the form of a cross-reference are fixed in the [contributor guidelines](../CONTRIBUTING.md#writing-documentation).

## Specify and check an inquiry

- [User guide](guide/_index.md): leads a researcher through the filling of the records, and states the commands that check them
- [Specification](specification/_index.md): documents the fields of each record of an inquiry, when each is required and the values that it admits
- [Epistemology](epistemology/_index.md): states the theory of inquiry behind the records, with the results of the philosophy of science that support each choice
- [Procedures](procedures/_index.md): guides the performance of an epistemic activity
- [Worked inquiries](../examples/_index.md): tests the framework on completed inquiry records and on adversarial research statements

## Decide a design question

- [Architecture decisions](adr/_index.md): records the design choices that later work must respect
- [Proposals](proposals/_index.md): compares the candidate designs of each open goal before a decision
- [Audits](audits/_index.md): keeps the assessments on which a decision rests until its resolutions are ratified

## Consult the external literature

- [References](references/_index.md): states on their own terms the external frameworks and literature that the decisions and the goals draw on
