- **A record at commitment `identified` fixes its referent before any quantity is declared.** The identification names the system or the class by a definite description, with pointers to the data, the results or the publications where it appears. At this commitment, a question can refer to the target, and an inquiry can start, before the declarations exist. The record reaches `specified` once its variables are declared and every symbol of its expressions resolves. The two levels and the checks that decide them are defined with the [levels of commitment](../epistemology/objects/question/question.md#^commitment-levels). ^target-commitment

- **The option "No external target" of the target type covers questions about a model class itself**: its consequences, regimes, limits and structural properties, under the [aim of knowledge of models](../epistemology/objects/task/epistemic-aim.md).

> [!IMPORTANT] No redundancy
> The reference class records only a name and, when needed, selection criteria, and restates none of the other specifications. No restatement is needed, because the other specifications together already form a schema. Each member of the class instantiates that schema with its own parameter values, as a theory specifies a family of models [winther2020].

- **Selection criteria are needed only when membership depends on an outcome of the dynamics**, for a formal class as for an empirical one. A restriction on prior properties, such as the species and brain area of recorded animals, belongs to the domain of the question that imposes it, as eligibility criteria define the population of a clinical trial [ich2019].

- **Each entry of a signature resolves to a constituent kind or to an index set, and the two cases are read differently.** An entry resolving to a constituent kind names the bearer of the quantity. In a system with several kinds of unit, such an entry therefore fixes which kind bears the quantity. An entry resolving to an index set, by contrast, names the set along which the quantity varies. A signature entry that resolves to neither is a *defect*.

- **A parameter or a variable names the signature that it takes**, with one entry per place, each a constituent kind or an index set. A quantity of a single unit names one constituent kind, and a quantity of a pair two. A quantity of the whole system names none. The places are generic: a connectivity weight defined over two neurons holds for any pair. A quantity whose single value holds for every tuple is marked as shared. A variable that is an aggregate is declared, in addition to its signature, with its defining map, "[variable] := [function of constituent variables]". ^places

- **An external system is fixed by its symbol, the variables that it shares, the constraints that it imposes on them, and its own quantities.** *Examples*: the temperature of a heat bath, the latent dimension of a generator of inputs, the weights of a decoder. Each such quantity is declared among the parameters or the exogenous variables of the record, and names its owner. One symbol table therefore serves the whole record, and a question narrows a quantity of an external system within its [domain](../epistemology/objects/subject/domain.md), as it does a parameter of $S$. A law of an external system, by contrast, is omitted when it involves no shared variable, because such a law cannot act on the target. ^external-parameters

- **A variant that no declaration carries names a member that the record cannot generate.** The other members remain describable, but that variant has no schema at all. The class therefore states a disjunction of variants, and the declarations support only some of its terms. A record may admit the gap by marking the variant open in its [maturity](maturity.md), the field reserved for such gaps. Otherwise, the gap is reported. To detect the gap, the check counts the entries that carry the variant, across every block of declarations in the record and in the background records that describe the target. Occurrences of the variant's name do not count, because that name recurs in its own prose and in the mark that admits the gap. ^unborne-variant

- The [difference between a variant and a domain restriction](../epistemology/objects/subject/target-system.md) grounds the field `variants`. The declarations nevertheless stay in one symbol table, because the resolver and every reference between records assume that layout. A variant partitions the symbol table, and its mark labels each entry carried by only some members. An entry left unmarked holds for the whole class, so it may not name a symbol that only some members have. Whether an entry names such a symbol is read from the fields through which its kind refers to symbols. An external system refers to it through the variables that it shares. A law of a [background record](../epistemology/objects/knowledge/background.md) refers to it through its expression, and a relation of the organization through the parts that it runs between and through its realizing laws. The mark itself must name a declared variant, because an undeclared mark releases its entry from holding for the whole class without assigning it to any member. ^variants

- **A constraint of an external system is a claim, and is recorded with the fields of a law.** The constraint can be false of the system and its environment together. The constraint nevertheless stays with the external system imposing it, not among the laws of a [background record](../epistemology/objects/knowledge/background.md), for two reasons. First, the block of external systems exists to state the boundary. Second, each admitted law bears on the target alone. ^external-constraint-shape

- **The role of a quantity fixes the field that declares it.** The [role test](../epistemology/vocabulary/quantities/variable-value-observable.md#^role-test) decides the role of a declared quantity within one phenomenon, and the role fixes its place in the records: ^quantity-roles

| Role | Place in the records |
| --- | --- |
| state variable | a variable of the target, with `determined: within`; for a parameter of the target, an entry of `course.varies` |
| exogenous variable | a variable of the target, with `determined: outside`; for a parameter of the target, an entry of `course.varies` |
| index of an observable, when the claim is a function of the values; source of variation, when the claim is a distribution over draws | a parameter of the target, or the initial value of a variable, named by the phenomenon among its indices or its sources of variation |
| parameter | a parameter of the target, named by neither |

- **The target declares a quantity by the course of its laws of evolution.** The target record declares a quantity as a parameter when the quantity stays constant along the course of the background laws with the role *evolution*, and as a variable otherwise. A phenomenon whose course makes a declared parameter vary lists that parameter in `phenomenon.course.varies`. *Example*: the weights $W$ of a network are parameters of the target, since its law of evolution keeps them fixed while the network relaxes to a fixed point. Training changes $W$, so the phenomenon of training lists $W$ in `course.varies`, where $W$ takes the role of a variable. ^declaration-rule
