# Maturity of a field

> [!QUESTION] Goal: how does a record mark the maturity of each of its fields?

The field `maturity` is carried by the records of the following kinds: target system, background, phenomenon, question, operators, model, binding, key, answer, assessment and problem; the itinerary record does not carry it.

Each specification may also carry its own maturity marker (open, provisional, or stabilized). A specification left unmarked takes the maturity that the status of its record states, for the records whose status is a maturity. In an answer, a problem or a question, whose status is an attitude or a stage, it takes the maturity `provisional`, as the [record attributes](../epistemology/objects/question/question.md#^record-attributes) state.

The reasons for admitting empty and immature fields are stated under the [safeguards against rigidity](../epistemology/objects/subject/subject-of-inquiry.md).
