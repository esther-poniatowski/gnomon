# Virtues of a question

> [!QUESTION] Goal: which virtues can a question name in the entries of its `virtues` block?

The [virtues](../epistemology/objects/task/epistemic-pragmatic-virtues.md) that a question may weigh, and the [standing of a virtue](../epistemology/objects/task/epistemic-pragmatic-virtues.md#^virtue-standing), are defined in the theory of epistemic and pragmatic virtues.

| Specification | When it is required | Options or frame | Examples |
| ------------- | ------------------- | ---------------- | -------- |
| Virtue | one entry per virtue that the inquiry weighs, under any epistemic aim | unification · generality · robustness · intelligibility · structural-transparency · economy · tractability | `intelligibility` |
| Standing | with every virtue | constitutive, when the product test applies the virtue · preferred, when appraisal ranks answers on it | `preferred`, when the virtue only decides between two adequate accounts |
| Justification | with every virtue | why the inquiry weighs the virtue, and the shortfall of an answer that lacks it | "an account that cannot be followed without the full calculation would not serve the design of experiments" |
