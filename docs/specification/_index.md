# Specification

Documents the fields of each record of an inquiry: the content that a researcher writes in each field, the condition under which the field is required, and the values that it admits.

> [!IMPORTANT] Specification and theory
> Each note documents one record kind, or one block of the question record, in the order of its template. The concepts behind the fields, and the grounds of each choice, are stated in the [epistemology](../epistemology/_index.md).

## Problem and subject

- [Problem record](problem.md): fills the record of the research problem that the questions serve
- [Target-system record](target-system.md): fills the record that delimits a system studied and declares its quantities
- [Background record](background.md): fills the record of the laws admitted about a target system
- [Phenomenon record](phenomenon.md): fills the record of a behavior of the target system that questions address

## Question

- [Question record](question.md): fills the fields that name the problem and the linked questions of a question
- [Subject of a question](question-subject.md): fills the `subject` block, with its contrast and its domain of conditions
- [Epistemic task of a question](question-task.md): fills the `task` block, from the epistemic aim to the proof obligations
- [Virtues of a question](question-virtues.md): chooses the virtues that a question names in its `virtues` block

## Answer and assessment

- [Model record](model.md): fills the record of a system studied in place of the target
- [Key record](key.md): fills the record of a map shared by the bindings of a model
- [Binding record](binding.md): fills the record that relates a model to a phenomenon
- [Answer record](answer.md): fills the record of a candidate answer, its core and its aim block
- [Assessment record](assessment.md): records the verdicts of the tests that a candidate answer passes or fails

## Itinerary and language

- [Itinerary record](itinerary.md): records the steps of an inquiry so that each step can be checked
- [Symbols and expressions](expressions.md): writes the symbols and the formulas of any record so that the checks resolve them
- [Operator record](operators.md): declares the operators that a problem adds to the canonical set
- [Maturity of a field](maturity.md): marks how far each field of a record has been revised
