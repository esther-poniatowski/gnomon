## Criteria

### Relevance kinds closed at design time ^t3-relevance-kinds-closed

The relevance-kind vocabulary is explicit, non-overlapping, and closed at design time. Initial proposed kinds: `logical`, `semantic`, `methodological`, `contextual`, `expository`, `historical`. The exact partition (especially the boundaries between semantic and methodological, contextual and expository) is a Tier-3 decision.
