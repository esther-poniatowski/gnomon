# Procedures

Guides the performance of an epistemic activity, once the [activity](../epistemology/processes/activities/_index.md) and its norm of success are fixed.

- [Procedures of an inquiry](inquiry/_index.md): guides the conduct of an inquiry as a whole, from its open conditions to its next act
- [Classification](classification/_index.md): guides the construction of a classification, from the principles that warrant it to the reduction of its features
