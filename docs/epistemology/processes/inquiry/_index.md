# Course of an inquiry

Orders the specification of an inquiry, decomposes its questions into the questions that serve them, and chooses its next act.

> [!WARNING] Notes with neighboring scopes
> - [Conditions of answerability](../../objects/question/conditions-of-answerability.md) states the conditions of an answer and their dependencies, whereas the [choice of the next act](choice-of-act.md) states the criteria by which an act that addresses them is chosen.
> - The [choice of the next act](choice-of-act.md) states those criteria, whereas the [procedure for choosing the next act](../../../procedures/inquiry/next-act.md) applies them to the records and maps each condition to the registered activities.
> - [Question decomposition](question-decomposition.md) states the sources, the types and the relevance of serving questions, whereas the [procedure for decomposing a question](../../../procedures/inquiry/decomposing-a-question.md) states the steps that find them and the register that keeps them.

- [Order of specification](specification-order.md): decides what to specify first, and how an earlier choice is revised without circularity
- [Question decomposition](question-decomposition.md): decides which questions serve a question, and when a decomposition must be revised
- [Choice of the next act](choice-of-act.md): decides which act of research can come next in a given state of an inquiry, and on which grounds one is preferred
