# Course of an inquiry

Orders the specification of an inquiry, diagnoses what an answer to each of its questions needs, and decomposes a question into the questions that serve it.

> [!WARNING] Notes with neighboring scopes
> - [Conditions of answerability](conditions-of-answerability.md) states the conditions of an answer and their dependencies, whereas the [choice of the next act](../../../procedures/inquiry/next-act.md) states the procedure that acts on them.

- [Order of specification](specification-order.md): decides what to specify first, and how an earlier choice is revised without circularity
- [Conditions of answerability](conditions-of-answerability.md): decides which conditions an answer to a question needs, and which of them can be established first
- [Question decomposition](question-decomposition.md): guides the decomposition of a question into the questions that serve it, as the inquiry proceeds
