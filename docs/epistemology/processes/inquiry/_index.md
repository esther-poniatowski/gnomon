# Course of an inquiry

Orders the specification of an inquiry, diagnoses what an answer to each of its questions needs, decomposes a question into the questions that serve it, and chooses the next act.

> [!WARNING] Notes with neighboring scopes
> - [Conditions of answerability](conditions-of-answerability.md) states the conditions of an answer and their dependencies, whereas the [choice of the next act](choice-of-act.md) states the criteria by which an act that addresses them is chosen.
> - The [choice of the next act](choice-of-act.md) states those criteria, whereas the [procedure for choosing the next act](../../../procedures/inquiry/next-act.md) applies them to the records and maps each condition to the registered activities.

- [Order of specification](specification-order.md): decides what to specify first, and how an earlier choice is revised without circularity
- [Conditions of answerability](conditions-of-answerability.md): decides which conditions an answer to a question needs, and which of them can be established first
- [Question decomposition](question-decomposition.md): guides the decomposition of a question into the questions that serve it, as the inquiry proceeds
- [Choice of the next act](choice-of-act.md): decides which act of research can come next in a given state of an inquiry, and on which grounds one is preferred
