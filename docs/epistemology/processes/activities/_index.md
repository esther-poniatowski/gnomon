# Epistemic activities

Types each operation of an inquiry by the epistemic object that it produces and by the norm that judges it.

> [!IMPORTANT] Activity, aim and method
> The notes of this folder type the operations of an inquiry. The [epistemic aim](../../objects/task/epistemic-aim.md) fixes the achievement served by those operations. The [overview of the taxonomy](epistemic-activities.md) separates an activity from an aim, a mode of inference and a method.

## Criterion and design

- [Epistemic activities](epistemic-activities.md): decides whether an operation is epistemic, and types it so that a research itinerary can be checked

## Families

- [Problem-posing](problem-posing.md): types the activities that turn a source into a problem and a problem into a question
- [Conceptual activities](conceptual-activities.md): types the activities that introduce, revise or operationalize a concept
- [Empirical activities](empirical-activities.md): types the activities that design an inquiry into a target and obtain data and phenomena from it
- [Model-building](model-building.md): types the activities that specify a target, admit its background laws and construct a model of it
- [Model analysis](model-analysis.md): types the activities that draw consequences holding of a model
- [Ampliative inference](ampliative-inference.md): types the inferences that reach a hypothesis beyond their premises
- [Evaluation](evaluation.md): types the activities that judge, weigh or rank candidate hypotheses and models
- [Explanation and integration](explanation-and-integration.md): types the activities that explain a phenomenon or relate theories to one another
- [Revision](revision.md): types the activities that change the attitude or the status of existing records
