#  Specifying a research question

> [!QUESTION] Goal: what must be fixed to specify a research question?

This [layer of inquiry](inquiry-specification.md) formulates the research question itself and names the objects under study.

A research question can be represented as:
$$Q=\langle S,P,X,\sigma\rangle $$

| Dimension                                     | Role                                                                                    | Example                                        | Canonical tradition             |
| --------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------- |
| **Target system $S$**                         | System or portion of reality about which knowledge is sought, that the model represents | neuron                                         | Philosophy of scientific models |
| **Phenomenon $P$** ("explanandum")            | Aspect about $S$ to be explained — a fact, event, pattern, property, behavior, etc.     | firing rate                                    | Hempel–Oppenheim                |
| **Contrast class $X$** (for a *why*-question) | Alternative(s) relative to which $P$ is puzzling — "why $P$ rather than $P'$?"          | why spike rather than inactivity?              | van Fraassen                    |
| **Scope/resolution $\sigma$**                 | Domain/granularity at which an answer is sought                                         | population-level, molecular, asymptotic, local |                                 |

*Example*: a well-posed *explanatory* research question has the form:

> In system $S$, why/how does $P$ occur rather than $X$, in terms of object at the $\sigma$ level?
