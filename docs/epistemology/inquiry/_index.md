# Inquiry

Organizes an inquiry above the level of one question, from the problem that it serves to the choice of its acts.

> [!WARNING] Notes with neighboring scopes
> - [Specifying an inquiry](inquiry-specification.md) states the layers into which the decisions of an inquiry fall. [Order of specification](specification-order.md) orders the filling of those layers and the revision of an earlier choice. [Question decomposition](question-decomposition.md) builds the questions that serve one question. [Choosing the next act](next-act.md) selects the act of research to perform once a problem is stated.
> - The [problem record](../../specification/problem.md) documents the fields of a problem, whereas [epistemic problem](epistemic-problem.md) states what a problem is and how its solution is checked.
> - [Conditions of answerability](conditions-of-answerability.md) states the conditions of an answer and their dependencies, whereas [choosing the next act](next-act.md) states the procedure that acts on them.

## Overview and order

- [Specifying an inquiry](inquiry-specification.md): locates each decision of a research project in the layer of the specification that holds it
- [Order of specification](specification-order.md): decides what to specify first, and how an earlier choice is revised without circularity

## Problem

- [Epistemic problem](epistemic-problem.md): decides when a situation of inquiry is a problem, states the problem so that its solution can be checked, and tells it from a question
- [Types of epistemic problem](problem-types.md): diagnoses why the demand of a problem is unmet, and reads off the aims, question forms and answer forms that the demand admits

## Question

- [Question](question.md): fixes how far each slot of a question commits to a referent, and links two questions
- [Question decomposition](question-decomposition.md): guides the decomposition of a question into the questions that serve it, as the inquiry proceeds
- [Conditions of answerability](conditions-of-answerability.md): decides which conditions an answer to a question needs, and which of them can be established first

## Inquiry procedures

- [Choosing the next act](next-act.md): decides which act of research to perform next, once a problem is stated
