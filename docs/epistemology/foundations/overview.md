# Overview of the theory

> [!QUESTION] Goal: which question does the theory of `gnomon` answer, and which epistemic objects, processes and commitments does its answer comprise?

## The question that the theory answers

The [README](../../../README.md#motivation) names five failures of a research project kept in prose: hidden dependencies, a diluted argument, drift toward side questions, mixed epistemic roles, and content held only in exposition. The five failures share one cause: prose serves both to store the content and to access it. The theory of `gnomon` therefore answers one root question: ^root-question

> How can a research inquiry be represented so that its content, the dependencies among its results, the warrant and the maturity of each result, and the next act that it calls for can be stated, checked and revised without any exposition, and then projected into expositions that add no content?

The theory answers the root question in three parts: the **epistemic objects** that an inquiry states, the **epistemic processes** by which it advances, and the **commitments** that the method makes about both. Each part is stated below in short accounts, and each account points to the note that holds its detail.

The theory covers concepts, distinctions, criteria stated over concepts, and the grounds of each choice in the literature. The theory does not cover the fields of the records, which the [specification](../../specification/_index.md) documents, nor the procedures that apply the theory, which the [procedures](../../procedures/_index.md) state. The [test that separates theory from specification](../../../CONTRIBUTING.md#theory-and-specification) decides the tier of each content.

## Epistemic objects

An inquiry states the following objects, each of which groups rows of the [inventory of epistemic objects](../objects/status/epistemic-objects.md#^inventory). The items follow the reading path below, and an item that uses a later one names it with a short gloss:

1. **Inquiry and state.** An [inquiry](inquiry-and-state.md#^inquiry) is a course of epistemic acts directed at problems, and its [state](inquiry-and-state.md#^state) at a given time holds the accepted knowledge $K$, the proposed candidates $H$, the open questions and the admitted background of each target.
2. **Problem.** A [problem](../objects/problem/epistemic-problem.md) is a demand for a result that $K$ does not yet deliver, posed from its sources, and its [obstacles](../objects/problem/problem-types.md) explain why the demand is unmet.
3. **Question and its specification.** A [question](../objects/question/question.md) is a request whose [possible answers](../objects/question/question.md#^answer-sets), fixed by its subject $\Sigma$ and its epistemic task $T$, form a definite set, and its [specification](../objects/question/question-specification.md) $\langle\Sigma,T,\mathcal{V}\rangle$ adds the virtues $\mathcal{V}$, which fix the standards of its answers.
4. **Subject.** The [subject of inquiry](../objects/subject/subject-of-inquiry.md) $\Sigma$ names the target system, the phenomenon, the contrast and the conditions held fixed, and its records declare the quantities, the data and the classes through which the target is observed.
5. **Admitted background.** The [background](../objects/knowledge/background.md) of a target holds the laws admitted about it before any answer is sought, which are [admitted, not necessarily accepted](inquiry-and-state.md#^admitted-accepted) into $K$.
6. **Task.** The [epistemic task](../objects/task/epistemic-task.md) $T$ fixes the kind of knowledge that an answer must deliver: its aim, its requested relation, its modal strength, its bound on the explanans, its required accuracy and its proof obligations.
7. **Virtues.** The [virtues](../objects/task/epistemic-pragmatic-virtues.md) $\mathcal{V}$ are the qualities by which a question compares its answers, each marked constitutive, which narrows the admissible answers, or preferred, which ranks them.
8. **Answer and its grounds.** An [answer](../objects/answer/answer-form.md) is a typed claim, a product of the inquiry and never part of the specification of its question, which rests on [grounds](../objects/knowledge/grounds-and-dependence.md#^ground), each with a role, and which becomes a result once it is accepted into $K$.
9. **Model system, binding, key and comparison.** When the route of an answer runs on a stand-in, the answer rests on a [model system](../objects/answer/model-and-binding.md), a binding relates that model to the phenomenon through a key, and a [comparison](../objects/answer/model-and-binding.md#^comparison), computed and never recorded, sets the model against the data.
10. **Assessment.** An [assessment](../objects/answer/satisfaction-conditions.md#^assessment) tests an answer for admissibility, warrant and appraisal, against conditions fixed before the answer is evaluated, and its disposition is computed from its verdicts.
11. **Concept.** A [concept](../objects/concept/concept.md) is a unit of meaning produced by explication, defined once and kept apart from the names and symbols that express it.
12. **Itinerary.** An [itinerary](../processes/activities/epistemic-activities.md#^itinerary) records each act of the inquiry as a step typed by its epistemic activity, so that its inputs and its output can be checked.
13. **Other products of activities.** The [product types](../processes/activities/epistemic-activities.md#^product-types) also comprise the design of observations, the consequence of a model, the relations between theories and the changes of attitude, each placed in the inventory.

## Epistemic processes

An inquiry advances by the following processes, listed in the order of one cycle of an inquiry. For each process, the [division of labour](commitments.md#^performed-recorded-judged) separates the acts that the tool performs, the contents that the records hold and the judgments of the researcher:

1. **Posing.** The [problem-posing](../processes/activities/problem-posing.md) activities turn a source into a problem, and a problem into the questions that serve it.
2. **Specifying in order.** The [order of specification](../processes/inquiry/specification-order.md) fixes the order in which a question is specified, and revises an earlier choice without circularity.
3. **Diagnosing answerability.** The [conditions of answerability](../objects/question/conditions-of-answerability.md) state the conditions that must hold before an answer can be obtained, and the order in which they can be established.
4. **Decomposing.** The [decomposition of a question](../processes/inquiry/question-decomposition.md) yields the questions that serve it, as the inquiry proceeds.
5. **Performing typed epistemic activities.** Each act instantiates one [epistemic activity](../processes/activities/epistemic-activities.md), typed by its inputs, its output and its norm.
6. **Assessing.** The [conditions of satisfaction](../objects/answer/satisfaction-conditions.md) return a verdict per test, against the version of the specification fixed before the assessment, and the disposition of the assessment is read from those verdicts.
7. **Accepting.** [Accepting](../objects/status/statuses-and-acceptance.md#^acceptance-act) an answer into $K$ is an act of the researcher, informed by the assessment and never computed from it.
8. **Revising.** The [revision](../processes/activities/revision.md) activities change the attitude toward existing claims or the frameworks that pose them, and every revision is [reported to the claims that depend on it](../objects/knowledge/grounds-and-dependence.md#^propagation).
9. **Choosing the next act.** The [choice of the next act](../processes/inquiry/choice-of-act.md) addresses an open or a refuted condition with an act that is ready, and ranks the ready acts, which the [procedure of the next act](../../procedures/inquiry/next-act.md) applies to a state, outside the theory tier.

## Notation and presentation

The concepts of a project are written in its [notation](../objects/concept/notation.md), which composes their symbols and serves research. The presentation of the content of an inquiry to a reader, through glossaries, audiences and expositions, is specified in `ekthesis`, as the [decision on the scope of gnomon](../../adr/research-scope.md) states. Presenting a result to a reader is not an epistemic process: it outputs a text, never a new epistemic object.

## Commitments

The [commitments of the method](commitments.md) are stated with their grounds in their own note:

1. [Mediated access](commitments.md#^commitment-access): an object is known only through a description, which is itself a representation.
2. [Literal claims, adequate declarations](commitments.md#^commitment-literal): a claim can be false of its target, whereas a declaration is assessed for its adequacy.
3. [Neutrality on existence](commitments.md#^commitment-neutrality): no record states whether its object exists independently of any description.
4. [Possible answers fixed by the subject and the task](commitments.md#^commitment-answers): the virtues fix only the standards of the answers.
5. [Tests fixed before evaluation, under iteration](commitments.md#^commitment-tests): each assessment is made against the version of the specification fixed before it.
6. [Three layers and a request, with the answer as a product](commitments.md#^commitment-layers): the subject, the task and the virtues specify a question asked under a request, and the answer is a product of the inquiry.
7. [Pluralism on evidence and on representation](commitments.md#^commitment-pluralism): severity and a Bayesian credence complement each other, and a binding records licensed inferences in a vocabulary chosen by the method.
8. [Warrant as provenance](commitments.md#^commitment-warrant): a result reports the laws to which it is exposed, never a degree of warrant.
9. [Base revision](commitments.md#^commitment-base): the accepted knowledge is a belief base.
10. [No inference at run time](commitments.md#^commitment-no-inference): no tool adds a claim, decides an acceptance or relabels a dependent.
11. [Fallibilism](commitments.md#^commitment-fallibilism): every specification of a record carries a maturity, with named exceptions, and every revision records its trigger.
12. [Least commitment](commitments.md#^commitment-least): a choice is deferred until a condition requires it.
13. [One source per unit](commitments.md#^commitment-one-source): a record names another unit by its identifier and never restates it.
14. [A role fixed by place](commitments.md#^commitment-role): the epistemic role of a unit follows from the object and the place that hold it.

## Reading path

The notes are read in the following order. Each note uses only terms defined before it, or terms that it links forward with a one-line gloss:

1. **Foundations.** This overview, then [inquiry and its state](inquiry-and-state.md), [existence and access](existence-and-access.md) and [things and constructs](things-and-constructs.md).
2. **Core vocabulary.** [Model](../vocabulary/representations/model.md), [claim and hypothesis](../vocabulary/representations/claim-and-hypothesis.md), [law](../vocabulary/regularities/law.md) and [pattern and phenomenon](../vocabulary/regularities/pattern-and-phenomenon.md). The other notes of the [vocabulary](../vocabulary/_index.md) serve for lookup, and the [notation](../vocabulary/notation/notation.md) is consulted throughout.
3. **Problem.** The [epistemic problem](../objects/problem/epistemic-problem.md).
4. **Question.** The [question](../objects/question/question.md), its [specification](../objects/question/question-specification.md) and its [conditions of answerability](../objects/question/conditions-of-answerability.md), then the [types of problem](../objects/problem/problem-types.md), whose obstacles use those conditions.
5. **Subject.** The notes of the [subject of inquiry](../objects/subject/_index.md), in the order of their index.
6. **Admitted background.** The [background](../objects/knowledge/background.md).
7. **Task and virtues.** The notes of the [epistemic task](../objects/task/_index.md), in the order of their index.
8. **Answer and assessment.** The [answer form](../objects/answer/answer-form.md), then [grounds and dependence](../objects/knowledge/grounds-and-dependence.md), then the other notes of the [answer and its assessment](../objects/answer/_index.md), in the order of their index.
9. **Statuses.** The [epistemic objects](../objects/status/epistemic-objects.md), then the [statuses and acceptance](../objects/status/statuses-and-acceptance.md).
10. **Concept.** The [concept](../objects/concept/concept.md), then the [notation of a project](../objects/concept/notation.md).
11. **Activities.** The [epistemic activities](../processes/activities/epistemic-activities.md), then their families in the order of their [index](../processes/activities/_index.md).
12. **Course of an inquiry.** The [order of specification](../processes/inquiry/specification-order.md), the [decomposition of a question](../processes/inquiry/question-decomposition.md) and the [choice of the next act](../processes/inquiry/choice-of-act.md), then the procedure of the [next act](../../procedures/inquiry/next-act.md).
13. **Commitments.** The [commitments of the method](commitments.md), which gather the commitments stated in the notes above, each with its grounds.
