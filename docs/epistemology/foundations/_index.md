# Foundations

States what the theory of `gnomon` is about and what it presupposes: the question that the theory answers, the inquiry and its state, and the stance of the method on the objects that it represents.

- [Overview of the theory](overview.md): orients a reader in the theory before any other note, and gives the order in which to read its notes
- [Inquiry and its state](inquiry-and-state.md): defines the inquiry whose state the records hold, and tells apart the statuses of that state
- [Existence and access](existence-and-access.md): separates the question of existence from the question of access, and states the stance of the method on each
- [Things and constructs](things-and-constructs.md): tells a feature of the world from a role fixed by a choice and from a representation, for each term of the theory
