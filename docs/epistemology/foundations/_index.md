# Foundations

States what the theory of `gnomon` is about and what it presupposes: the question that the theory answers, the inquiry and its state, the stance of the method on the objects that it represents, and the commitments of the method.

> [!WARNING] Notes with neighboring scopes
> - The [overview of the theory](overview.md) lists each commitment in one line, whereas the [commitments of the method](commitments.md) state each commitment with its grounds.
> - [Existence and access](existence-and-access.md) states the stance of the method on access, claims and existence, which the commitments of the method gather with the others.

- [Overview of the theory](overview.md): orients a reader in the theory before any other note, and gives the order in which to read its notes
- [Inquiry and its state](inquiry-and-state.md): defines the inquiry whose state the records hold, and tells an accepted claim from an admitted claim and from an obtained claim
- [Existence and access](existence-and-access.md): separates the question of existence from the question of access, and states the stance of the method on each
- [Things and constructs](things-and-constructs.md): tells a feature of the world from a role fixed by a choice and from a representation, for each term of the theory
- [Commitments of the method](commitments.md): justifies each commitment of the method, and separates the acts that the tool performs from the contents that the records hold and from the judgments of the researcher
