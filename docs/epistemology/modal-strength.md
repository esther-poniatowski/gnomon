# Specifying the modal strength

> [!QUESTION] Goal: what must be fixed to specify how tightly an answer must bind the phenomenon?

The modal strength is the third dimension of the [epistemic task](epistemic-task.md), the layer that fixes the kind of knowledge that an answer delivers. This dimension fixes the claim that a candidate answer must establish about its explanans. Three claims are admitted: the explanans holds of the system, it could hold, or no alternative could hold.

| Modal strength              | Claim the answer must establish                                                                          | Evidence route                                                                                     | Source                                                               |
| --------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **actual** (how-actually)   | the explanans is *realized* in the target system, and *actually* produces the phenomenon there           | measuring or intervening on the system, or, for a formal target, deriving the explanans from its laws | [craver2006]; [verreaultjulien2019]                                  |
| **possible** (how-possibly) | the explanans is *consistent* with the laws of the system and the domain, and *would* produce the phenomenon | exhibiting a model that satisfies those laws and yields the phenomenon                       | [craver2006]; [verreaultjulien2019]                                  |
| **necessary**               | *no alternative* consistent with those laws produces a foil                                      | impossibility proof, or exhaustive exclusion over the class of alternatives that those laws admit                               | [mathematical derivation](requested-relation.md), as a requested relation |

- **How-possibly and how-actually differ by the *claim* that a model supports.** A model provides evidence for a claim, either a possible explanation or an actual one [verreaultjulien2019]. One model supports either claim, depending on the part of its content established for the target.
	
- **Constraints move an answer along the continuum from how-possibly to how-actually.** A how-possibly model conjectures components and activities without showing that they occur in the target. Empirical constraints, once added, move the model towards a how-actually answer [craver2006].
	
- **The laws of the system fix which possibility is at stake.** A possibility claim states that the explanans is compatible with the [laws of the target system](target-system.md#^constituents) and with the [conditions the question holds fixed](domain.md). Without those laws and conditions, "possible" has no determinate content.
	
- **The target type bounds the modal strength** (proposal). A hypothetical target, or a class of merely possible systems, supports no claim of *actual* strength about an existing system. A question that pairs such a [target type](target-system.md) with the actual strength is therefore inconsistent.
	
- **The modal strength and the aim of modal knowledge belong to two different dimensions of the task** (proposal). The [epistemic aim](epistemic-aim.md) of modal knowledge makes a possibility or an impossibility the product of the inquiry. The modal strength instead qualifies how tightly the answer of any aim must bind its phenomenon.
