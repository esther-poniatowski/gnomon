# Specifying the modal strength

> [!QUESTION] Goal: what must be fixed to specify how tightly an answer must bind the phenomenon?

The modal strength is the third dimension of the [epistemic task](epistemic-task.md). This dimension fixes what a candidate answer must establish about its explanans: that the explanans holds of the system, that it could hold, or that no alternative could.

| Modal strength              | What the answer must establish                                                                           | Evidence route                                                                                     | Source                                                               |
| --------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **actual** (how-actually)   | the explanans is *realized* in the target system, and *actually* produces the phenomenon there           | measurement or intervention on the system; for a formal target, derivation from its laws | [craver2006]; [verreaultjulien2019]                                  |
| **possible** (how-possibly) | the explanans is *consistent* with the laws of the system and the domain, and *would* produce the phenomenon | exhibit a model that satisfies those laws and yields the phenomenon                        | [craver2006]; [verreaultjulien2019]                                  |
| **necessary**               | *no alternative* consistent with those laws produces a foil                                      | impossibility proof, or exhaustive exclusion over the class they admit                               | [requested relation](requested-relation.md): mathematical derivation |

- **How-possibly and how-actually differ by the claim, not by the model.** A model provides evidence, while the claim it supports is either a possible explanation or an actual one [verreaultjulien2019]. The same model supports either claim, depending on how much of it has been established of the target.
	
- **Constraints move an answer along the continuum.** A how-possibly model conjectures components and activities without showing that the target contains those components. Adding empirical constraints moves it towards a how-actually answer [craver2006].
	
- **The laws of the system fix which possibility is at stake.** A possibility claim states that the explanans is compatible with the [laws of the target system](target-system.md#^constituents) and with the [domain](domain.md). Without those, "possible" has no determinate content.
	
- **The target type bounds the modal strength** (proposal). A hypothetical target, or a class of merely possible systems, supports no actual claim about an existing system, so pairing such a [target type](target-system.md) with the actual strength is inconsistent.
	
- **Modal strength is not the modal-knowledge aim** (proposal). The [epistemic aim](epistemic-aim.md) of modal knowledge makes a possibility or an impossibility the product of the inquiry. The modal strength instead qualifies how tightly the answer of any aim must bind its phenomenon.
