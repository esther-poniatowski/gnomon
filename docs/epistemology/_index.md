# Epistemology

States the theory of inquiry on which the records of `gnomon` rest: the epistemic objects that an inquiry states, the processes by which it advances, the commitments of the method, and the grounds of each choice in the literature.

> [!IMPORTANT] Where to start
> A reader starts with the [overview of the theory](foundations/overview.md), which states the question that the theory answers and the reading path through its notes.

> [!WARNING] Theory and specification
> A note of this folder states a concept, a distinction or the grounds of a choice. The fields that a record holds are documented in the [specification](../specification/_index.md).

- [Foundations](foundations/_index.md): states what the theory is about, what it presupposes, and which commitments the method makes
- [Objects of inquiry](objects/_index.md): defines the epistemic objects that an inquiry states, and the statuses that those objects bear
- [Processes of inquiry](processes/_index.md): defines the processes by which an inquiry advances, from its epistemic activities to the course of its questions
- [Vocabulary](vocabulary/_index.md): fixes the senses of the terms that the theory uses, and the symbols of its notation
