# Epistemology

Fixes the content that a researcher must specify in each [record](../../src/gnomon/data/templates/_index.md) of an inquiry, and the results of the philosophy of science that support each choice.

- [Inquiry](inquiry/_index.md): organizes an inquiry above the level of one question, from the problem that it serves to the record of its acts
- [Subject of inquiry](subject/_index.md): fixes the objects that a research question is about
- [Epistemic task](epistemic-task/_index.md): fixes the kind of knowledge that an answer must deliver, and the qualities by which admissible answers are compared
- [Answer and assessment](answer/_index.md): fixes the form of a candidate answer and the tests that decide whether it succeeds
- [Epistemic activities](activities/_index.md): types each operation of an inquiry by its inputs, its output and its norm of success, so that an itinerary can be checked
- [Record language](language/_index.md): fixes the conventions by which the method notes and the records write their symbols and formulas
- [Definitions](definitions/_index.md): fixes the term and the sense that name an element of an inquiry, and tells the term apart from its neighbors
