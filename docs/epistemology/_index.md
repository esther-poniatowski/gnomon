# Epistemology

States the theory of inquiry on which the records of `gnomon` rest: the concepts and the distinctions of an inquiry, the syntheses that `gnomon` draws from the literature, and the grounds of each choice.

> [!WARNING] Theory and specification
> A note of this folder states a concept, a distinction or the grounds of a choice. The fields that a record holds are documented in the [specification](../specification/_index.md).

- [Inquiry](inquiry/_index.md): organizes an inquiry above the level of one question, from the problem that it serves to the choice of its acts
- [Subject of inquiry](subject/_index.md): defines the objects that a research question is about
- [Epistemic task](epistemic-task/_index.md): defines the kind of knowledge that an answer must deliver, and the qualities by which admissible answers are compared
- [Answer and assessment](answer/_index.md): defines a candidate answer and the tests that decide whether it succeeds
- [Epistemic activities](activities/_index.md): types each operation of an inquiry by its inputs, its output and its norm of success, so that an itinerary can be checked
- [Notation](notation/_index.md): registers the symbols of the theory notes
- [Definitions](definitions/_index.md): fixes the term and the sense that name an element of an inquiry, and tells the term apart from its neighbors
