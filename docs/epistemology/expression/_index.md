# Expression

Separates the content of an inquiry from the names, symbols and texts that express it for a reader.

> [!WARNING] Notes with neighboring scopes
> - [Glossary and notation](names-and-symbols.md) states the forms that express the concepts of a research project, whereas the [concept](../objects/concept/concept.md) states their meaning, and the [vocabulary](../vocabulary/_index.md) fixes the senses of the terms in which the theory itself is written.
> - The notes of this folder state the concepts and their grounds, whereas the decisions on [concepts, glossaries and notations](../../adr/concepts-glossaries-notations.md) and on [expositions as projections](../../adr/expositions-as-projections.md) fix the records that realize them.

- [Glossary and notation](names-and-symbols.md): decides which names and symbols express the concepts of one project, and how a notation composes its symbols
- [Exposition](exposition.md): decides what an exposition may add to the research that it presents to an audience
