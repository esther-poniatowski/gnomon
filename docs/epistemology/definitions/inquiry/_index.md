# Problems and questions

Fixes the senses of the terms that name the problem and the question of an inquiry, and the criteria that separate those terms.

> [!WARNING] Notes with neighboring names
> [Conditions of answerability](conditions-of-answerability.md) states the conditions of an answer and their dependencies, whereas [choosing the next act](../../inquiry/next-act.md) states the procedure that acts on them.

- [Epistemic problem](epistemic-problem.md): decides when a situation of inquiry is a problem, states the problem so that its solution can be checked, and tells it from a question
- [Types of epistemic problem](problem-types.md): diagnoses why the demand of a problem is unmet, and reads off the aims, question forms and answer forms that the demand admits
- [Conditions of answerability](conditions-of-answerability.md): decides which conditions an answer to a question needs, and which of them can be established first
- [Problem and question](problem-and-question.md): fixes how far each slot of a question commits to a referent, and links two questions
