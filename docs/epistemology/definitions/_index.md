# Definitions

> [!QUESTION] Goal: which term, in which sense, names each element of an inquiry, and by which criterion is it told apart from its neighbors?

> [!IMPORTANT] Terms and specifications
> A note of this folder fixes the senses of a term and the criteria that separate it from neighboring terms. A specification note of the [epistemology](../_index.md) fixes the content that a record must state about one element of an inquiry. To fill a field, a reader opens the specification note. To choose between two terms or two senses, a reader opens the note of this folder.

> [!WARNING] Notes with neighboring names
> - [Mechanism and mechanistic model](mechanism-and-mechanistic-model.md) fixes the senses of the term and the test of a mechanistic model, whereas the [specification of a mechanistic answer](../mechanism.md) fixes the aspects required of such an answer.
> - [Pattern and phenomenon](pattern-and-phenomenon.md) fixes the senses of the terms, whereas the [specification of the phenomenon](../phenomenon.md) fixes the fields required of a record of a phenomenon.
> - [Model](model.md) separates a model system from its target, whereas the [specification of the target system](../target-system.md) fixes the fields of the target specification, the record that delimits the target.
> - [Condition](condition.md) fixes the senses of the term, whereas the [domain of conditions](../domain.md) is one of the places of a record that hold conditions.
> - [Conditions of answerability](conditions-of-answerability.md) states the conditions of an answer and their dependencies, whereas [choosing the next act](../next-act.md) states the procedure that acts on them.

## Framing

- [Things and constructs](things-and-constructs.md): to decide whether a term names a feature of the world, a role fixed by a chosen boundary, or a representation.

## Problems and questions

- [Epistemic problem](epistemic-problem.md): to decide when a situation of inquiry is a problem, to state the problem so that its solution can be checked, and to tell it from a question.
- [Types of epistemic problem](problem-types.md): to diagnose why the demand of a problem is unmet, and to read off the aims, question forms and answer forms that the demand admits.
- [Conditions of answerability](conditions-of-answerability.md): to decide which conditions an answer to a question needs, and which of them can be established first.
- [Problem and question](problem-and-question.md): to fix how far each slot of a question commits to a referent, and to link two questions.

## Bearers and systems

- [Entity, object and thing](entity-object-thing.md): to name the bearer of a description at the right degree of generality.
- [System, constituent and environment](system-constituent-environment.md): to assign each object of a description its role relative to a boundary.
- [Property, attribute, predicate and relation](property-attribute-predicate.md): to tell a feature of a bearer from the concept and the expression that represent it.
- [Kind, class and population](kind-class-population.md): to fix the collection over which a law, a model or a claim generalizes.

## Quantities and states

- [Variable, value, observable, parameter and data](variable-value-observable.md): to identify the formal object introduced by a declaration of a quantity.
- [Scale and level](scale-and-level.md): to decide which statements about values are meaningful, and which sense of scale or level a statement uses.
- [State, state space and configuration](state-and-configuration.md): to check whether a set of values forms a complete state.
- [Condition](condition.md): to assign each given constraint of an inquiry its role and its place in a record.
- [Invariant and symmetry](invariant-and-symmetry.md): to state which transformations a claim of invariance refers to.

## Change

- [Process, event and history](process-event-history.md): to classify a predicate on a declared course as a state, a process or an event.
- [Activity](activity.md): to tell a mechanistic activity from a process, an interaction, an event, a function and a dynamic.
- [Dynamic](dynamic.md): to tell the rule of change from the process, the law and the mechanism.
- [Interaction and cause](interaction-and-cause.md): to tell a causal interaction from a statistical one, and a relation from an interaction.
- [Function](function.md): to choose between the causal-role and the etiological sense of the function of a component.
- [Computation](computation.md): to tell the function computed from the algorithm, the implementation and the process of computing.

## Regularities and explananda

- [Law](law.md): to tell a pattern from its statement, and a law statement from an accidental generalization.
- [Pattern and phenomenon](pattern-and-phenomenon.md): to tell a regularity from a spatial or temporal pattern, and to decide when either counts as a phenomenon.
- [Capacity and disposition](capacity-and-disposition.md): to state a capacity as a conditional with a criterion of performance, apart from a function and an activity.
- [Mechanism and mechanistic model](mechanism-and-mechanistic-model.md): to choose a definition of mechanism, and to decide when a mechanistic model explains.
