# Regularities and explananda

Fixes the senses of the terms that name a regularity, the explanandum of an inquiry and the mechanism that explains it, and the criteria that separate those terms.

> [!WARNING] Notes with neighboring names
> - [Mechanism and mechanistic model](mechanism-and-mechanistic-model.md) fixes the senses of the term and the test of a mechanistic model, whereas the [specification of a mechanistic answer](../../mechanism.md) fixes the aspects required of such an answer.
> - [Pattern and phenomenon](pattern-and-phenomenon.md) fixes the senses of the terms, whereas the [specification of the phenomenon](../../phenomenon.md) fixes the fields required of a record of a phenomenon.

- [Law](law.md): tells a pattern from its statement, and a law statement from an accidental generalization
- [Pattern and phenomenon](pattern-and-phenomenon.md): tells a regularity from a spatial or temporal pattern, and decides when either counts as a phenomenon
- [Capacity and disposition](capacity-and-disposition.md): states a capacity as a conditional with a criterion of performance, apart from a function and an activity
- [Mechanism and mechanistic model](mechanism-and-mechanistic-model.md): chooses a definition of mechanism, and decides when a mechanistic model explains
