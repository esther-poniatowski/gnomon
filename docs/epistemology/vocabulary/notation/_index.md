# Notation

Registers the symbols by which the theory notes name the objects of the method.

> [!WARNING] Notes with neighboring scopes
> - [Notation](notation.md) registers the symbols of the theory notes themselves, whereas the [symbols and expressions](../../../specification/expressions.md) of the specification fix how a record declares its own symbols and refers to them in formulas.
> - [Notation](notation.md) registers the symbols of the theory notes, whereas the [notation of a project](../../objects/concept/notation.md) states what the notation of one research project holds, the symbols that express its own concepts.

- [Notation](notation.md): finds the object that a symbol of the theory denotes, and the note that declares it
