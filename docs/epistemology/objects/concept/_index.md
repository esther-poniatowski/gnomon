# Concept

Defines the concepts that a project uses to describe its objects, and the notation that composes their symbols.

> [!WARNING] Notes with neighboring scopes
> - [Concept](concept.md) states the meaning that a project fixes, whereas the [notation of a project](notation.md) states the symbols that designate it.
> - A concept belongs to the research of a project, whereas the [vocabulary](../../vocabulary/_index.md) fixes the senses of the terms in which the theory itself is written.

- [Concept](concept.md): defines a concept as a unit of meaning that an inquiry produces, and keeps it apart from its names
- [Notation of a project](notation.md): composes the symbols of a project from declared parts, apart from the concepts and quantities that they designate
