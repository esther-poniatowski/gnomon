# Problem

Defines the problem that opens an inquiry, the conditions under which it exists, and the obstacles that keep its demand unmet.

> [!WARNING] Notes with neighboring scopes
> - The [problem record](../../../specification/problem.md) documents the fields of a problem, whereas [epistemic problem](epistemic-problem.md) states what a problem is and how its solution is checked.
> - The [types of epistemic problem](problem-types.md) diagnose why a demand is unmet, whereas the [conditions of answerability](../question/conditions-of-answerability.md) define the conditions whose refutation the second axis of that diagnosis records.

- [Epistemic problem](epistemic-problem.md): decides when a situation of inquiry is a problem, states the problem so that its solution can be checked, and tells it from a question
- [Types of epistemic problem](problem-types.md): diagnoses why the demand of a problem is unmet, and reads off the aims, question forms and answer forms that the demand admits
