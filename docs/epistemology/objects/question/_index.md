# Question

Defines a question, the specification that fixes its admissible answers, and the links between questions.

- [Question](question.md): decides what counts as a question, how far a slot of a question form may stay uncommitted, and how two questions are linked
- [Specification of a question](question-specification.md): locates each decision about a question in the layer of its specification, and derives what those layers fix for its answers
