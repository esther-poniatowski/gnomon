# Derivation

States why a consequence holds and how it was reached, so that a derivation conveys the meaning and the relevance of its steps beyond their validity.

> [!WARNING] Notes with neighboring scopes
> - The [units of research work](../../processes/units-of-work.md) decide which record holds a fact of a piece of work, whereas the [derivation](derivation.md) defines the record that holds the reasoning below the grain of a product.
> - The [orientation of a move](orientation.md) states why a move is relevant at its point, whereas the [warrant of a move](move.md) states why it is admissible, and an [interpretation](interpretation.md) states the meaning of an item.
> - A [scheme](segment-and-scheme.md) states a general way of deriving, whereas the [pattern of a derivation](consequences-and-understanding.md) records a derivation as one instance of a scheme, with the other problems within reach of that scheme.

- [Derivation](derivation.md): decides what a derivation is, and which parts its record states
- [Sought of a derivation](sought.md): states what a derivation seeks, including a result that it cannot state in advance
- [Posits and their tests](posit.md): decides which premises a derivation may introduce on trial, and when a consequence that rests on them holds
- [Moves and warrants](move.md): defines one step of a derivation, its license, and the steps required in a record
- [Orientation of a move](orientation.md): identifies the next relevant move of a derivation before that move is made
- [Interpretation in a derivation](interpretation.md): classifies the meanings assigned to the items of a derivation, and the ground of each meaning
- [Segments and schemes](segment-and-scheme.md): groups the moves of a derivation under one idea, and reuses a way of deriving across derivations
- [Consequences, impasses and understanding](consequences-and-understanding.md): records the outcomes of a derivation, and the understanding gained beyond them
