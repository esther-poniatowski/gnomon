# Audit - Model design (Pilot)

> [!QUESTION] Goal: do the records of the [decision on answers, models and bindings](../adr/answers-models-bindings.md) hold four contrasting inquiries, and which resolutions of the [synthesis](model-design-synth.md) do these inquiries ratify, amend or leave open?

The pilot migrates four worked inquiries to the revised answer, the model record and the binding, in the order of the goal `model-design` of the [project goals](../../TODO.md). Each finding below names the inquiry that raised it, the defect or the open point, and its resolution, adopted or proposed. The decision absorbs the adopted resolutions when the pilot ends, and this note is then retired with the other audits.

## Neural tangent kernel

The inquiry states a theorem about a formal class, so its route runs on the target and its answer declares a use instead of a binding.

- **The block of model knowledge restated the claim.** The block held an expression and a reading of the consequence, which the claim of the core already states. *Adopted resolution*: the block keeps the form of the consequence and the obligations that it discharges, and the consequence is the claim of the core. The aim is renamed `model-properties`, since the former label echoed `modal-knowledge` and read as knowledge drawn from a model.
- **The constants of a bound belong to the manifestation.** The synthesis left open whether the constant of a bound is a feature of the manifestation or a derived phenomenon. The migrated phenomenon declares each constant as a feature of the manifestation that holds its bound, beside the bound, its horizon and its mode of convergence. *Adopted resolution*: a constant of a bound is a feature of its manifestation, so the answer introduces no quantity of its own.
- **The checks read no answer and no model.** The vocabulary check read neither record kind, so an undeclared symbol in a claim passed every test. *Adopted resolution*: the check reads both kinds. An answer resolves its symbols against the records on which its route runs and against the question that it answers, with its target system, its phenomenon and its backgrounds; a model resolves against its parents and its background.
- **The expressions had no connectives.** A claim and a domain conjoin conditions, and the syntax admitted no conjunction. *Adopted resolution*: the canonical operators gain `and`, `or` and `not`, written infix or as a call.
- **The checks of realizations read the former key.** The check of a link read `realizations` at the root of the answer, whereas the revised answer holds them in its claim. *Adopted resolution*: the check reads `claim.realizations`.

## Active matter

The inquiry explores the phases of a formal class, on a model derived from the target by an idealization that removes the noise and an approximation near onset.

- **A derived model of a formal target needs no binding.** The claim bears on a formal class, and the operations of the derived model carry its relation to the target, with their classes of error. *Adopted resolution*: the answer declares its use, the binding test is not applicable, and the robustness test reads the errors of the operations.
- **The answer classifies the operations against its target.** The model types its two operations against its parent: `idealize` and `approximate`. Against the stochastic target, the answer reads the removal of the noise as an idealization and the linearization near onset as a condition of its domain. *Adopted resolution*: this division ratifies the rule of ownership of the synthesis under the decision, since only an answer classifies an assumption against its target.
- **A catalogue is a claim, not a law of the model.** The former answer stated the catalogue of phases as a law of role `measurement`. The revised answer states it as its claim, which reaches the manifestation of the phenomenon. *Adopted resolution*: this ratifies the answer that holds claims only.
- **An operation is a unit that a ground names.** A ground of the answer names an operation of the model by its identifier, which the reference check did not resolve. *Adopted resolution*: a reference reaches a unit by its symbol or by its identifier, for the operations and the assumptions of a model, the grounds and the abstractions of an answer, and the steps and the rules of a design.
- **The checks of a derived model.** *Adopted resolution*: two checks join the synthesis checks: an expression of a derived model uses no symbol that an operation retires, and each operation states its bound when its error is controlled, its regime when it is an approximation, and its order when it is a singular limit.
- **The requirements of a model assume a root model.** The registry requires constituents, variables and laws of every model, whereas a derived model keeps the symbols of its parents visible and declares only those that its operations yield. The migrated model declares no constituent. *Open point*: the requirements of a derived model, to settle when the checks read the requirements of models.

## Grokking

The inquiry explains the delay of generalization on an effective theory of representation learning, which four operations derive from the target: a restriction to the toy addition task, an aggregation of the parameters into a representation, the limit of an infinitesimal decoder step, and an extension by a posited relaxation law. The question names outputs to match, so the model is bound to the phenomenon, although the target is a formal class.

- **A question with outputs to match requires a binding, even on a formal target.** The fidelity test compares the values of the model with the outputs of the phenomenon, and only a binding states the pairs that the comparison reads. *Proposed resolution*: an answer on a model other than the target needs a binding whenever its question names outputs to match, whether or not the target is a formal class; it declares a use only when its claim bears on the model or the class alone and its question names no output.
- **A model names no quantity of the phenomenon.** The former answer stated the timescale law over the times of fitting and generalization that the phenomenon declares. The model now declares its own delay, which the binding pairs with the delay of the phenomenon. *Adopted resolution*: a model relates to the phenomenon through its binding only, as the decision states.
- **A restriction states its subclass.** The answer derives its domain from the restriction to the toy addition task only if the operation states that subclass. *Adopted resolution*: the regime of a restriction states the subclass as an expression, here over a parameter that the model declares for the task.
- **The target statuses follow from the operations.** The slow relaxation holds only in the limit of the decoder step, so it is a limit construct; the nonzero mode describes the representation, an aggregate, so it is a coarse-graining. Each factor names the pair of the binding that carries it. *Adopted resolution*: the operations of the derived model decide the status of a factor, which the test of target status reads.
- **The warrant of a placement is no law.** A limit construct requires a ground of role `warrant` that states why the target falls in the regime of the limit, whereas the table of ground roles restricts a warrant to a law of a model or of a background. *Open point*: widen the role `warrant` to an argued pair of a binding, or give the placement a role of its own.
- **A limit may have an uncontrolled error.** The note on derived models admitted a controlled or a singular error for a limit, whereas the limit of the decoder step has no known correction. *Adopted resolution*: a limit is controlled when a correction is known, otherwise uncontrolled, or singular.
- **The checks read bindings.** *Adopted resolution*: a binding resolves its expressions against its model, its phenomenon and the target system of that phenomenon, and its pairs are units that a factor names by their identifiers.

## Tipping cascades

The inquiry forecasts transition sequences of the Earth system from a stylized network of four tipping elements, whose elements are units of the model alone. Two shared keys and one binding carry the model onto the target.

- **A stylized model is a root model with units of its own.** The former answer entered the subsystems of the target by `reference`, which asserted an identity that the model never has with its target. The model now declares its own elements, and a component pair of the binding carries them onto the subsystems. *Adopted resolution*: this ratifies the end of `reference` for a stylized constituent, as the decision states.
- **The verdict of individuation follows from the binding.** The component pair carries the four elements one to one onto the four subsystems, by their labels, so the individuation test reads the pair. *Adopted resolution*: this meets the fourth condition of acceptance of the goal `model-design`.
- **A key serves several pairs.** The key of the tipped state carries both the probability and the distribution of the time of each element, and the map of units carries the prescribed warming onto the global warming through a condition pair. *Adopted resolution*: this ratifies the shared key of the decision.
- **The references to whole records were not checked.** A misspelled identifier of a key passed every test, and the new check found a broader concept that no record stated. *Adopted resolution*: every reference to a whole record must name a record, and the missing broader concept is now stated.
- **An argued pair again stands as a warrant.** The correspondence of the elements is a ground of role `warrant` that names an argued pair, as in the grokking inquiry. *Open point*: the same as for the grokking inquiry.
