# Registries

The registries of this folder declare the epistemic activities, the products that they take and produce, and the conditions of answerability on which they act. The itinerary check reads the first two, so that each step of an [itinerary record](../../../../docs/specification/itinerary.md) can be compared with the activity that it instantiates, and `gnomon status` reads the third.

- [Epistemic activities](activities.yml): to find the input and output types, the norm, the mode and the context of an activity before writing a step of an itinerary.
- [Product types](product-types.yml): to find which record kind holds a product of a given type, and which types no record kind holds yet.
- [Conditions of answerability](answerability.yml): to find, for each condition of answerability, the conditions on which it depends and the activities that act on it.
- [Words of the statement frame](statements.yml): to find the words of each clause of the statement frame.
- [Rules of a derivation](derivations.yml): to find the admitted pairs of an interpretation, the kinds of a non-routine move and the tests that confirm a posit.
- [Base dimensions](dimensions.yml): to find the base dimensions that a dimensional formula may name.
