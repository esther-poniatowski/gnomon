# Project goals

This list holds the goals of `gnomon` that span several parts of the project.

## Frameworks

- [ ] Build a framework that formalizes definitions, grounded in the authoritative literature of epistemology and checked against concrete problems of `geonexus`. Its product is the full definition object to which a concept record may link, following the [decision on concepts, glossaries and notations](docs/adr/concepts-glossaries-notations.md); the next action is a note of the [epistemology](docs/epistemology/_index.md) that specifies this object. Its [candidate formalisms](docs/proposals/definitions-framework.md) await adoption or decline. 🆔 definitions-framework
- [ ] Decide whether the activities of the [registry of activities](src/gnomon/data/registries/activities.yml) are primitive, or derive from a smaller set of constructors, and state the answer in the [note on epistemic activities](docs/epistemology/processes/activities/epistemic-activities.md). Its [candidate formalisms](docs/proposals/research-actions.md) await adoption or decline. 🆔 research-actions
- [ ] Build a framework that formalizes derivation (the analysis), its consequences, and synthesis (the answer). It extends the [answer form](docs/epistemology/objects/answer/answer-form.md). Its [candidate formalisms](docs/proposals/derivation-framework.md) await adoption or decline. 🆔 derivation-framework
	- [ ] Account for each assumption of a derivation: a later step discharges it, the conclusion absorbs it as a stated hypothesis, or it stays open as a debt that a check reports. Record also the commitments that a statement presupposes without asserting them, each with its strength: a local role, a provisional commitment, or an axiom of the whole corpus.
	- [ ] Decide whether a reusable reasoning scheme, which holds slots, a warrant template, critical questions and conditions of application, becomes a unit of its own apart from its filled instances, under the [admission of a record kind](docs/adr/record-kind-admission.md#^admission-conditions). The critical questions of a scheme supply sub-questions, and a methodological argument, which justifies the choice of a method, is one candidate scheme.
	- [ ] Report the idle units of a derivation, from which no path of the dependency graph reaches the conclusion, and decide whether an avoidable detour is measured on that graph, compared with a shorter admissible route, or judged by the author.
	- [ ] Record what a derivation gains, at three grains: the gain of each step, for which the [grammars for the rationale of a step](docs/proposals/research-corrections.md#grammars-for-the-rationale-of-a-step) propose a field; the principle that explains several cases at once; and the pattern of the derivation that transfers to a nearby case within stated variations.
	- [ ] Separate the model of an answer from its consequences, so that a derived law is a consequence, with one derivation record per answer, as the [kernel and segments of a derivation](docs/proposals/derivation-framework.md#kernel-segments-and-defects-of-a-derivation) propose.
- [ ] Generate the export of a project: its research units, their symbols and their relations, each relation mapped onto the classes of the contract that `ekthesis` publishes, as the [decision on the scope of gnomon](docs/adr/research-scope.md) states. 🆔 research-export

Generate the index from the schemas of the goal `record-schemas`, whose fields carry a title and a description. Once the index exists, decide whether it replaces the option lists of the specification notes and of the templates, as the [decision on one source per unit](docs/adr/one-source-per-unit.md#^vocabulary-copies) leaves open. ⏫ 🆔 field-index

## Integration of the Geonexus pilot

Its generic decisions on research records belong to `gnomon`, its decisions on exposition records belong to `ekthesis`, as the [decision on the scope of gnomon](docs/adr/research-scope.md) states, and `geonexus` keeps only its bindings and the application to the pilot. The decisions are listed in the [pilot handoff](https://github.com/esther-poniatowski/geonexus/blob/42d8a238991105b3c1640a69d5bfe27c8db703e0/projects/mixture-input-encoding/HANDOFF.md), and drafted in the decision notes [argument-architecture records](https://github.com/esther-poniatowski/geonexus/blob/42d8a238991105b3c1640a69d5bfe27c8db703e0/docs/_decisions/argument-architecture-records.md), [research pilot profile](https://github.com/esther-poniatowski/geonexus/blob/42d8a238991105b3c1640a69d5bfe27c8db703e0/docs/_decisions/research-pilot-profile.md) and [exposition pilot profile](https://github.com/esther-poniatowski/geonexus/blob/42d8a238991105b3c1640a69d5bfe27c8db703e0/docs/_decisions/exposition-pilot-profile.md) of `geonexus`.

## Open issues of the framework trials

The trials of the [worked inquiries](examples/_index.md) left candidate changes of the shared schema, decisions local to some cases, and limits of the checks. The diagnosis behind each candidate, and the earlier decisions of the trials, remain in the git history: `test/framework-issues-synthesis.md` and `test/framework-corrections-handoff.md` at commit a4fb1b4.

- [ ] Give each candidate change of the shared schema a review state: approved, declined or deferred. 🆔 trial-candidates
	- [ ] Scope each virtue: a virtue of the [question template](src/gnomon/data/templates/question.yml) names only a virtue, a standing and a justification, so it names neither the conclusion that it qualifies nor the declared variations across which that conclusion is tested. The [virtues note](docs/epistemology/objects/task/epistemic-pragmatic-virtues.md) owns the change, which succeeds when two assessors apply one virtue to the same declared object and scope.
	- [ ] Record the provenance of each commitment: add optional source identifiers to the technical entries of the templates other than laws, which carry `origin`, and a bibliography to each record, while `phenomenon.data` keeps only the evidence for the phenomenon. The [problem template](src/gnomon/data/templates/problem.yml) already carries `sources`, and the [binding template](src/gnomon/data/templates/binding.yml) its `calibration_provenance`. The change succeeds when a reviewer traces each commitment without reconstructing its origin from the surrounding prose.
	- [ ] Decide whether `scope_over_instances` nests inside each manifestation, since a manifestation is the unit of assertion. In the [phenomenon of delayed generalization](examples/grokking-delayed-generalization/phenomenon.yml), the ordering of the two crossings holds for every run, whereas the delay is an ensemble claim over initializations, so one scope for the whole record cannot qualify both claims. The [note on the phenomenon](docs/epistemology/objects/subject/phenomenon.md) owns the decision.
	- [ ] Check the option cells of the specification notes against the [vocabulary registry](src/gnomon/data/vocabularies.yml), so that each cell becomes an [admitted copy](docs/adr/one-source-per-unit.md#^vocabulary-copies). The item waits on the decision of the goal `field-index` whether the generated field index replaces the option cells, and lapses if it does.
- [ ] Make the local decisions that the issues notes of the [active-matter](examples/active-matter-phase-exploration/issues.md), [delayed-generalization](examples/grokking-delayed-generalization/issues.md) and [tipping-cascade](examples/earth-system-tipping-cascades/issues.md) inquiries still require, without supplying any unknown scientific result. 🆔 case-decisions
