# Project goals

This list holds the goals of `gnomon` that span several parts of the project, grouped in families in the order of their priority. Tracking the activity of research operationally is the key objective of `gnomon`, and the views turn that tracking into a global perspective on a project that proceeds by local increments; the other families serve these two.

## 1. Operational tracking of research activity

The records represent each act of an inquiry as the act is taken, so that the course of research is tracked and the next act can be computed.

- [ ] Represent every act of an inquiry operationally in its records: close the gaps of the graph of activities, keep each itinerary valid as the records change, and compute the next act. The second review of the records found that a restart of a project can be written but not fully tracked. 🆔 inquiry-records-round-2
	- [ ] Close the gaps of the type graph of activities, as the [choice of the next act](docs/procedures/inquiry/next-act.md) marks them in its map from conditions to activities and in its tables of acts:
		- [ ] develop a proof technique or an algorithm that gives access to a quantity, whose product the [record of a technique](docs/proposals/inquiry-records-round-2.md#record-of-a-technique) proposes; an algorithm is a design of kind `method`, and a proof technique waits on the decision of `derivation-framework` on reusable reasoning schemes.

## 2. Global views

A researcher who proceeds by local increments needs the global state of a project, which the views compute from the records.

- [ ] Generate views of the state of an inquiry from its records, beside the [question tree](src/gnomon/views.py), so that a researcher reads the global state of a project without opening each record. Its [candidate views](docs/proposals/inquiry-views.md) await adoption or decline. 🆔 inquiry-views
	- [ ] Emit each view as a stable YAML record, which Ekphrasis renders in Obsidian as a table, a tree or a graph, and decide which renders stay dynamic as the records change.
	- [ ] Emit the question tree of `gnomon tree` as such a record, and decide whether the Markdown table of `gnomon vocabulary` gives way to a render of its YAML record.
- [ ] Build the view of a filled inquiry record: compose the question statement from its fields, and typeset each expression in LaTeX from the notation that the [operator registry](src/gnomon/data/operators.yml) and the `operators.yml` of each problem already hold. The [syntax decision](docs/adr/expression-syntax.md) fixes the input notation; only the view of a vocabulary is built so far. 🆔 record-view
	- [ ] Correct the comment of `statement` in the [question template](src/gnomon/data/templates/question.yml), which names a command `gnomon render` that does not exist: name the view of this goal instead. The consequence of the [decision on grammatical fields](docs/adr/grammatical-fields-and-readings.md) that cites this comment then needs the same correction.

## 3. Research frameworks

The frameworks formalize the acts that the records track: definition, derivation and synthesis, the statement of a problem, and classification.

- [ ] Build a framework that formalizes definitions, grounded in the authoritative literature of epistemology and checked against concrete problems of `geonexus`. Its product is the full definition object to which a concept record may link, following the [decision on concepts, glossaries and notations](docs/adr/concepts-glossaries-notations.md); the next action is a note of the [epistemology](docs/epistemology/_index.md) that specifies this object. Its [candidate formalisms](docs/proposals/definitions-framework.md) await adoption or decline. 🆔 definitions-framework
- [ ] Build a framework that formalizes derivation (the analysis), its consequences, and synthesis (the answer). It extends the [answer form](docs/epistemology/objects/answer/answer-form.md). Its [candidate formalisms](docs/proposals/derivation-framework.md) await adoption or decline. 🆔 derivation-framework
	- [ ] Account for each assumption of a derivation: a later step discharges it, the conclusion absorbs it as a stated hypothesis, or it stays open as a debt that a check reports. Record also the commitments that a statement presupposes without asserting them, each with its strength: a local role, a provisional commitment, or an axiom of the whole corpus.
	- [ ] Decide whether a reusable reasoning scheme, which holds slots, a warrant template, critical questions and conditions of application, becomes a unit of its own apart from its filled instances, under the [admission of a record kind](docs/adr/record-kind-admission.md#^admission-conditions). The critical questions of a scheme supply sub-questions, and a methodological argument, which justifies the choice of a method, is one candidate scheme.
	- [ ] Report the idle units of a derivation, from which no path of the dependency graph reaches the conclusion, and decide whether an avoidable detour is measured on that graph, compared with a shorter admissible route, or judged by the author.
	- [ ] Record what a derivation gains, at three grains: the gain of each step, for which the [grammars for the rationale of a step](docs/proposals/research-corrections.md#grammars-for-the-rationale-of-a-step) propose a field; the principle that explains several cases at once; and the pattern of the derivation that transfers to a nearby case within stated variations.
	- [ ] Separate the model of an answer from its consequences, so that a derived law is a consequence, with one derivation record per answer, as the [kernel and segments of a derivation](docs/proposals/derivation-framework.md#kernel-segments-and-defects-of-a-derivation) propose.
- [ ] Extend the problem record to the [formal statement of a problem](docs/epistemology/objects/problem/epistemic-problem.md): the remaining checks of its obstacles, as stated by the [types of problem](docs/epistemology/objects/problem/problem-types.md). The demand is typed by `result_type` and `request`, and the record states its knowledge obstacles and its answerability obstacles. 🆔 problem-record
	- [ ] State the criterion by which the [closure of the admitted laws](docs/epistemology/objects/knowledge/background.md#^laws) makes a target a formal class rather than a class of possible systems, which the [types of problem](docs/epistemology/objects/problem/problem-types.md) apply without defining; record in the [problem record](docs/specification/problem.md) that formality is read from that closure, so that the problem kind admits no value `formal`.
	- [ ] Decide whether the field `addresses` of a question becomes required.
	- [ ] Compare the answerability obstacles that a problem states with the refuted conditions of its associated question, once the question record holds the state of each condition, and decide whether ill-defined terms can be read from the records, since a specified record of a term is necessary for its definiteness but not sufficient.
- [ ] Complete the method of the activity `classification`: the [construction of a qualitative feature system](docs/procedures/classification/construction-procedure.md), and the taxonomic layer built on it, whose routes the [extension from a feature system to a taxonomy](docs/proposals/classification-method.md) compares. The next action is the review of the global scope contract. 🆔 classification-method
	- [ ] Review the global scope contract, which the [feature-system output boundary](docs/procedures/classification/construction-procedure.md#^scope-output-boundary) and the extension plan implement, and check that its wording states the three intended boundaries: the output stops before taxa, the formal guarantees hold for finite crisp tables only, and each dependency names its representation.
	- [ ] Once the scope contract is approved, specify the assumptions and the guarantees of each formal operation.
	- [ ] Once the formal contracts are specified, correct each procedure and its statement of complexity, then update the worked examples.
	- [ ] Once the procedures are corrected, reconcile the terminology and the navigation, then close the review markers `==FIX: …==` of the procedure, each after its correction is approved.
	- [ ] Choose the route that governs the taxonomic layer, among those that the extension plan compares.

The trials of the [worked inquiries](examples/_index.md) left candidate changes of the shared schema and decisions local to some cases; their diagnosis remains in the git history, in `test/framework-issues-synthesis.md` and `test/framework-corrections-handoff.md` at commit a4fb1b4.

- [ ] Align the registries, the templates and the checks with the revised theory. 🆔 theory-code-alignment
	- [ ] Let an assessment reference the version of the question against which it was made, so that a revision of a layer can void the verdicts made under an earlier version, as the [rule on tests fixed before evaluation](docs/epistemology/objects/answer/satisfaction-conditions.md#^tests-fixed-before) requires.
- [ ] Repair the notes and the terms that the second review of the records found inconsistent. 🆔 note-repairs

	- [ ] Reclassify the representation geometry in the [note on representation and encoding](docs/epistemology/vocabulary/representations/representation-and-encoding.md) as a property of the encoding map (factor `law-property`), whose occurrence may be a process along training; the answer of the [neural tangent kernel inquiry](examples/neural-tangent-kernel-model-properties/answer.yml) follows.

## 6. Guidance for researchers

A researcher new to `gnomon` needs a procedure that leads, step by step, from a research problem to records that the checks accept.

- [ ] Write a tutorial that walks through one worked inquiry stage by stage, from the problem to the assessment, with the itinerary step and the command of each stage. 🆔 tutorial
- [ ] Explain in the [guide to filling the records](docs/guide/filling-records.md) how to choose identifiers, what the field `_schema` names, how to mark the maturity of a field, and which specifications each level of commitment and each aim require. 🆔 guide-conventions
- [ ] Index the fields of every record in one generated file, `docs/guide/field-index.md`, registered in the [user guide](docs/guide/_index.md), in alphabetical order of their paths, nested paths included, each linked to the anchor of the note that documents it. Generate the index from the schemas of the goal `record-schemas`, whose fields carry a title and a description. Once the index exists, decide whether it replaces the option lists of the specification notes and of the templates, as the [decision on one source per unit](docs/adr/one-source-per-unit.md#^vocabulary-copies) leaves open. ⏫ 🆔 field-index

## 7. Checks

- [ ] Close each known limit of the checks, or state it in the note that owns the rule. 🆔 check-limits
	- [ ] Run the corpus tests of `tests/test_gnomon/test_symbol_resolution.py` and `test_inquiry_records.py` through the orchestration of `src/gnomon/check.py`, so that the context of each checker is assembled in one place; the tests still assemble it themselves.
	- [ ] Decide whether a parser enforces the expression grammar, as the [syntax decision](docs/adr/expression-syntax.md) anticipates. The check of `vocabulary.py` is lexical: it resolves identifiers without parsing binders. A parser would close two limits:
		- the signature of a derived observable is declared and not derived from its definition, so a record can declare a signature that its definition contradicts;
		- a domain limit such as `Re -> infinity` is one string whose arrow no operator defines, so no check verifies that its left side is a declared quantity of the target;
	- [ ] Restrict the count of unused declarations, `unused_declarations` in `src/gnomon/vocabulary.py`, to grammatical fields. The count reads the whole text of the records, readings and comments included, so a symbol named only in a reading counts as used, against the [rule on prose fields](docs/adr/grammatical-fields-and-readings.md#^prose-opaque). The next action is to collect the text from the values of the fields that a grammar declares.
	- [ ] Exclude the generated `vocabulary.yml` from the records that `load_problem` in `src/gnomon/vocabulary.py` reads, as `unused_declarations` already excludes it, following the [decision on the inputs of checks](docs/adr/checks-read-records.md#^checks-read-records). In the seven worked inquiries the table contributes no declaration, operator or interaction, so no verdict depends on it yet.
	- [ ] Bind the free dimension variable of `grad`, `div` and `lap` in the [operator registry](src/gnomon/data/operators.yml) to the domain over which they run. The type language of the [expression language](docs/specification/expressions.md) cannot yet state that binding.
	- [ ] Check the places of each relation of an `organization` in `vocabulary.py`. A relation that names fewer places than its carrier passes, since the check requires only that every place index the carrier. A relation whose pairs are reversed passes as well, since both endpoints index the carrier either way.
	- [ ] Verify whether the `signature` check misses a quantity applied at a literal index.
	- [ ] Check that a law marked derived follows from its premises. The warrant of a law propagates to every result resting on that law, so a warrant that no check confirms records only an intention.
	- [ ] Check that each constitutive virtue is settled by the product test and each preferred virtue by appraisal, each exactly once, as the [conditions of satisfaction](docs/epistemology/objects/answer/satisfaction-conditions.md) require.
	- [ ] Check the conditions stated in the column *When it is required* of the specification notes, which the [requirement registry](src/gnomon/data/requirements.yml) does not hold, such as an index set required when neither a constituent kind nor a parameter supplies the set; the lists `always`, `by_commitment` and `by_aim` of the registry are already checked.
	- [ ] Check the order of the keys within an entry, which the [record templates](src/gnomon/data/templates/_index.md) state and no test enforces.
	- [ ] Give a detection test a symbol once an assessment reports a verdict per detection test. No field references a detection test yet.
	- [ ] Give `gnomon.cli` a `__main__` guard, or state in the [command reference](docs/guide/cli-reference.md) that the module form of the command is `python -m gnomon`. Invoked as `python -m gnomon.cli`, the command imports the module, exits 0 and performs no action.
	- [ ] Exercise in the worked inquiries the fields that no case fills: the `claim.realizations` of an answer, once the migration under `model-design` moves the key, and the inhibiting conditions, nonstandard circumstances and byproducts of a phenomenon. These instances need quantities that no case declares yet, such as a dissipation rate for the turbulent anomaly or an intervention scale for a nonstandard preparation.

## 8. Integration with the ecosystem

Its generic decisions on research records belong to `gnomon`, its decisions on exposition records belong to `ekthesis`, as the [decision on the scope of gnomon](docs/adr/research-scope.md) states, and `geonexus` keeps only its bindings and the application to the pilot. The decisions are listed in the [pilot handoff](https://github.com/esther-poniatowski/geonexus/blob/42d8a238991105b3c1640a69d5bfe27c8db703e0/projects/mixture-input-encoding/HANDOFF.md), and drafted in the decision notes [argument-architecture records](https://github.com/esther-poniatowski/geonexus/blob/42d8a238991105b3c1640a69d5bfe27c8db703e0/docs/_decisions/argument-architecture-records.md), [research pilot profile](https://github.com/esther-poniatowski/geonexus/blob/42d8a238991105b3c1640a69d5bfe27c8db703e0/docs/_decisions/research-pilot-profile.md) and [exposition pilot profile](https://github.com/esther-poniatowski/geonexus/blob/42d8a238991105b3c1640a69d5bfe27c8db703e0/docs/_decisions/exposition-pilot-profile.md) of `geonexus`.
