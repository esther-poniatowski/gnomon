# Project goals

This list holds the goals of `gnomon` that span several parts of the project, grouped in families in the order of their priority. Tracking the activity of research operationally is the key objective of `gnomon`, and the views turn that tracking into a global perspective on a project that proceeds by local increments; the other families serve these two.

## 1. Operational tracking of research activity

The records represent each act of an inquiry as the act is taken, so that the course of research is tracked and the next act can be computed.

- [x] Represent every act of an inquiry operationally in its records: close the gaps of the graph of activities, keep each itinerary valid as the records change, and compute the next act. The second review of the records found that a restart of a project can be written but not fully tracked. 🆔 inquiry-records-round-2
	- [x] Close the gaps of the type graph of activities, as the [choice of the next act](docs/procedures/inquiry/next-act.md) marks them in its map from conditions to activities and in its tables of acts:
		- [x] develop a proof technique or an algorithm that gives access to a quantity: an algorithm is a design of kind `method`, made by `method-design`, and a proof technique a design of kind `scheme`, made by `scheme-design`, whose norm is the soundness of its conditions of application, as the [decision on design records](docs/adr/design-record.md) states.
		- [x] fill and revise the fields of the epistemic task: `task-specification` fixes them once the subject is declared, and `question-revision` changes them after a failure, as the [problem-posing activities](docs/epistemology/processes/activities/problem-posing.md#^task-specification) state.

## 2. Global views

A researcher who proceeds by local increments needs the global state of a project, which the views compute from the records.

- [x] Decide whether the Markdown table that `gnomon vocabulary --markdown` writes gives way to a render of its YAML record by Ekphrasis, as the views of `gnomon views` do: every computed display is a YAML view, the vocabulary binds the schema `gnomon:view.vocabulary@2`, and the Markdown table is retired. 🆔 vocabulary-view
- [x] Build the view of a filled inquiry record: typeset each expression in LaTeX from the notation that the [operator registry](src/gnomon/data/operators.yml) and the `operators.yml` of each problem already hold. The view `expressions` of `gnomon views` holds the form of each expression, composed by the [LaTeX module](src/gnomon/latex.py). 🆔 record-view

## 3. Research frameworks

The frameworks formalize the acts that the records track: definition, derivation and synthesis, the statement of a problem, and classification.

- [ ] Build a framework that formalizes definitions, grounded in the authoritative literature of epistemology and checked against concrete problems of `geonexus`. Its product is the full definition object to which a concept record may link, following the [decision on concepts, glossaries and notations](docs/adr/concepts-glossaries-notations.md); the next action is a note of the [epistemology](docs/epistemology/_index.md) that specifies this object. Its [candidate formalisms](docs/proposals/definitions-framework.md) await adoption or decline. 🆔 definitions-framework
- [x] Build a framework that formalizes derivation (the analysis), its consequences, and synthesis (the answer). It extends the [answer form](docs/epistemology/objects/answer/answer-form.md). The [theory of derivations](docs/epistemology/objects/derivation/_index.md) states its parts, the [decision on the derivation record](docs/adr/derivation-record.md) fixes its record, and the [specification of the record](docs/specification/derivation.md) documents its fields. The [decision on the content of a claim](docs/adr/claim-content.md) settles its presuppositions and its modality. 🆔 derivation-framework
	- [x] Write the specification of the derivation record, its base schema, its template and its contract `gnomon:derivation@1`, with the registries of its closed vocabularies: the kinds of move, of posit and of test, the intended changes of a purpose, the reasons for setting an alternative aside, and the items and kinds of interpretation with their admitted pairs.
	- [x] Amend the [specification of a design](docs/specification/design.md): a scheme of derivation is a design of the kind `scheme`, with its conditions of application, its posits, and a situation and a patch for each mode of failure, as the [decision on design records](docs/adr/design-record.md) states.
	- [x] Point the route of an answer and the obligations of an assessment to derivation records, and let the vocabulary check read a derivation through its [visible tables](docs/adr/flat-symbol-table.md#^visible-tables).
	- [x] Add the checks of a derivation, each with a planted defect: the kernel test, the order of information of the orientations, the admitted pairs and the ground types of the interpretations, the bridge of each segment, and the exposure of a derivation in the view of exposure.
	- [x] Decide which of the defects of a derivation, idle units included, a check reports from the units of the record, as the [decision on the derivation record](docs/adr/derivation-record.md#defects-that-a-check-reports) states.
	- [x] Record a worked derivation in the [inquiry into delayed generalization](examples/grokking-delayed-generalization/_index.md): the construction of the law that sets the scale of the delay.
	- [x] Record the commitments that a statement presupposes without asserting them, each in its one home: the question, the condition of a quantity, the claim, or a relation `presupposes` between answers, as the [presuppositions of a claim](docs/epistemology/objects/knowledge/presupposition.md) place them.
	- [x] Decide the two candidates left open by the proposals for the derivation framework: the algebraic data types for propositional content, and the modality of a statement, as the [decision on the content of a claim](docs/adr/claim-content.md) states.
- [x] Implement the [dimensional formula of a quantity](docs/adr/dimensional-formula.md): the optional attribute `dimensional_formula` in the specification and the schemas of quantities, the base dimensions in package data and the base scales in the operator record of a problem, a rule of dimension for each operator of the registry, and the homogeneity check, tested on planted laws that fail it. 🆔 dimensional-formula

- [ ] Extend the problem record to the [formal statement of a problem](docs/epistemology/objects/problem/epistemic-problem.md): the remaining checks of its obstacles, as stated by the [types of problem](docs/epistemology/objects/problem/problem-types.md). The demand is typed by `result_type` and `request`, and the record states its knowledge obstacles and its answerability obstacles. 🆔 problem-record

- [ ] Complete the method of the activity `classification`: the [construction of a qualitative feature system](docs/procedures/classification/construction-procedure.md), and the taxonomic layer built on it, whose routes the [extension from a feature system to a taxonomy](docs/proposals/classification-method.md) compares.
