	- [ ] Account for each assumption of a derivation: a later step discharges it, the conclusion absorbs it as a stated hypothesis, or it stays open as a debt that a check reports. The critical questions of a scheme supply sub-questions, and a methodological argument, which justifies the choice of a method, is one candidate scheme.
