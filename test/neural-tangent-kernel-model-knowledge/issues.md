# Current assessment of the neural-tangent model-knowledge specification

## Result

The current records express the requested model consequences as three proof obligations. Each conclusion names one discrepancy bound and requires the bound to vanish with width. Each manifestation retains the bound and its convergence specification. A class-member scope leaves the validity domain open.

## Global issues

The requirement registry adds no structured product for `model-knowledge`. The neural-tangent question uses `proof_obligations` because its requested products are theorems, but the validator would also accept a model-knowledge question that omitted them. The aim should require either proof obligations or another explicit consequence specification.

The domain limit remains one expression plus a justification. The components of each convergence claim are consequently distributed across the justification and the referenced phenomenon features and proof obligations. A structured limit reference could connect these fields directly.

## Deliberately open science

The quantitative convergence specification and its theorem assumptions are the requested results. They remain open because the cited theorems establish different claims under different assumptions; none applies uniformly to the entire declared class.

No candidate artifact is supplied.
